Artwork

Mixed-media and whimsical art

In addition to her work in film and acting, Diana is an accomplished visual artist specializing in mixed-media and whimsical art.

She previously owned and operated an art gallery in Miami, Florida, and her artwork has been collected and sold to buyers around the world.

Her background as an artist continues to influence her filmmaking, particularly her approach to visual storytelling, composition, color and character.
